# Customer Segmentation Dashboard

## Dashboard Objective

Provide a concise business view of customer segments, customer value, revenue contribution, and recommended strategic focus.

## Dashboard Sections

### 1. Executive KPIs
- Total Customers
- Total Revenue
- Average Revenue per Customer
- Number of Segments

### 2. Customer Segment Distribution
- Customers by Segment
- Customer Share by Segment

### 3. Revenue Contribution
- Revenue by Segment
- Revenue Share by Segment

### 4. Segment Profile
- Recency
- Orders
- Revenue
- Average Order Value
- Product Diversity

### 5. Business Strategy
- Segment
- Business Objective
- Recommended Action
- Primary KPI

## Data Sources

- `data/processed/customer_segmentation_final.csv`
- `data/processed/segment_eda_summary.csv`
- `data/processed/customer_revenue_concentration.csv`
- `data/processed/segment_comparison.csv`


## Dashboard Principle

The dashboard should prioritize business decisions and segment differences rather than displaying every available metric.
